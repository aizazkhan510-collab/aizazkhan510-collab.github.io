Privacy Policy

App: YouTube Agent
Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YouTube Agent ("the app", "we") collects, uses and protects information when you sign in with your Google account.

1. Information we access

When you sign in with Google, the app may request access to:

  • Basic profile information (name, email address, profile picture).
  • YouTube data you authorise, such as your channel details, videos, playlists and related metadata, through the YouTube API Services.

We only request the permissions needed for the features you use.

2. How we use information

  • To sign you in and identify your account.
  • To perform the YouTube actions you request inside the app.
  • To improve and troubleshoot the app.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except as required by law.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Other services the app uses

To produce videos, the channel owner can optionally use AI writing and voice services (Anthropic Claude for scripts; ElevenLabs or Google Cloud Text-to-Speech for narration) or a free voice model that runs entirely in the browser (Kokoro, downloaded from public code and model hosting sites). Only the episode topic and script text are sent to these services. No YouTube or Google account data is ever sent to them.

6. Data storage and retention

The app has no server and no database. Your Google access token is kept only in your browser's memory for the current session and is never sent anywhere except Google's own APIs. Settings and keys you choose to "remember" stay in your own browser's local storage.

Access tokens and data are stored only as long as needed to provide the app's features. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

7. Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time from Google Account permissions.

8. Children

This app is a tool used only by the channel owner; children do not use it. The channel's videos are designated "Made for Kids" on YouTube, and the app collects no information about viewers, including children who watch them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue or contact us via github.com/aizazkhan510-collab.
